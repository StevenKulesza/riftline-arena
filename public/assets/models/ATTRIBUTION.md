# Character asset

`quaternius-swat.glb` is **SWAT** by Quaternius, distributed by Poly Pizza
under the CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain dedication. The downloaded GLB was
validated to contain four named humanoid armor meshes and 24 embedded
combat/locomotion animation clips before integration.

- Source: https://poly.pizza/m/Btfn3G5Xv4
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

`combat-trooper.glb` is **Sci-Fi Soldier / Futuristic Combat Trooper** by
ART_LOLL, distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. The supplied
Sketchfab GLB contains six static PBR mesh/material zones and no skeleton or
animation clips. Riftline generates humanoid skin weights at load time and
uses the CC0 SWAT skeleton and motion library above as an animation donor; the
legacy SWAT meshes are not rendered.

- Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sci-fi-soldier-futuristic-combat-trooper-de876bfdce1c47a4aa67670faee7208e
- Author: https://sketchfab.com/evgenytvidov
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

# Flamethrower Drone asset

`flamethrower-drone.glb` is **Flamethrower Drone** by Jonas Prunskus, distributed
under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. The supplied Sketchfab GLB contains the
authored hard-surface body and PBR textures; its embedded showcase ground plane
is removed at runtime. The source has no skeleton or animation clips, so the
in-game spider locomotion uses a deterministic articulated runtime rig.

- Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/flamethrower-drone-6bc6e70b2c6b467d95f4d7e1756e116f
- Author: https://sketchfab.com/JPrun
