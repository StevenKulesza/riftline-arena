# Riftline Arena — Weapon Concept Set

AAA hard-surface concept sheets generated as visual development and 3D-modeling references for the seven weapons defined in `src/game/config.ts`.

## Shared design language

- Original near-future arena-sport hardware with a graphic, faceted silhouette.
- Believable PBR construction: layered armor, machined metal, ceramic panels, rubber contact surfaces, glass energy cells, fasteners, seams, service panels, cables, cooling hardware, and subtle battle wear.
- Dark contact masses with one gameplay-readable emissive color per weapon.
- Each landscape sheet combines a large three-quarter presentation view with consistent orthographic side, top, front, and rear references.
- Construction is intended to be physically modelable, with visible part breaks, thickness, bevels, material separation, grip ergonomics, and muzzle/rear mechanisms.

## Weapon prompts

### VX Machine Gun

Compact cyan bullpup machine gun with a heavy faceted receiver, four-barrel rotary cluster in a protective collar, rear magazine, ammo window, ejection port, charging handle, top rail, feed cable, cooling slots, ribbed grip, pale-cyan ceramic armor, and cyan emissive indicators.

### Scatter-14

Compact amber double-barrel pump shotgun with two large parallel bores, broad receiver, ribbed sliding pump, three barrel clamps, side shell rack, breach latch, front sight, heat ports, orange ceramic inlay, bronze metalwork, and amber emissive details.

### Rift Rocket

Coral shoulder-fired launcher built around a concentric tube, massive front mantlet, recessed bore, luminous muzzle ring, flared rear venturi, six exhaust vanes, five structural collars, skeletal side rails, top spine, rangefinder, guidance cell, shoulder struts, and fire-control cable.

### Ion Plasma

Violet energy weapon with a compact receiver, two forward emitter prongs cradling a glass plasma reactor, bright capsule core, five containment rings, luminous conduits, coolant lines, bilateral capacitor banks, toroidal shield, and a restrained emitter crown.

### Helix Laser

Slim green beam carbine with a faceted receiver, pale mint shell, twin emitter prongs, three graduated focusing rings, small central emitter, bilateral radiator fins, side power pack, transparent charge window, fiber bus, and lime emissive optical path.

### Longshot

Magenta precision rifle with a low-profile stock, adjustable cheek rest, rubber butt pad, ceramic receiver, long barrel, four floating heat-shroud rings, angular scope on twin brackets, elevation and windage controls, bolt handle, compact magazine, heat fins, and ported muzzle brake.

### Apex Railgun

Rare acid-yellow power weapon with a central spine, two long separated acceleration rails, seven clamps per side, luminous live rails, open firing channel, glass flux cell, capsule core, five containment rings, twin flux-bus cables, capacitor banks, rear field cage, and semicircular muzzle bridge.

## Generation method

Generated with the built-in image generation tool using the `stylized-concept` workflow. These sheets are visual-development references rather than dimensionally exact engineering drawings; lock final scale and orthographic proportions during the blockout pass.
